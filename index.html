Who we are

Every child deserves a strong start

Goal4 Initiative is a non-profit organisation committed to accelerating learning outcomes among ECDE learners and children with disabilities in rural communities in Kenya.

We believe strong educational foundations shape the rest of a child’s life. When children get the right support early, they gain the confidence, skills and opportunity to thrive.

  • Better early learning environments
  • No child with a disability left behind
  • Teachers who are trained, equipped and supported

Our mission & pillars

Two pillars, one big dream

Both pillars focus on the earliest years of learning, one of the most important times in a child’s life.

Pillar 1

TAJI · ECDE Strengthening

“Taji” means crown

We support early childhood centres with learning materials, teaching aids, teacher support and safe learning spaces, so every child can learn, grow and thrive.

Explore TAJI
Pillar 2

INUKA · Disability Inclusion

“Inuka” means rise up

We support children with disabilities with assistive learning resources, teacher training and inclusive classrooms, so every child can learn, grow and thrive.

Explore INUKA
