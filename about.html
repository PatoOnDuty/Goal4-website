Foundations that shape the future

Goal4 Initiative is a Kenyan non-profit working to improve learning for early childhood (ECDE) learners and children with disabilities in rural communities.

Who we are

We believe in early starts

We believe strong educational foundations shape the rest of a child’s life. When children get the right support early, they gain the confidence, skills and opportunity to thrive.

Our work strengthens early learning environments and makes sure children with disabilities are not left behind.

Our name comes from UN Sustainable Development Goal 4: making sure every child gets an inclusive, fair and good-quality education.

What drives us

Our mission & values

Mission

To speed up learning for ECDE learners and children with disabilities in rural Kenya.

Vision

A Kenya where every child, whatever their ability, gets a strong start in learning.

Inclusion

Every child is seen, supported and included in the learning journey.

Community

We work hand in hand with schools, teachers, parents and partners.

The journey so far

Bringing back possibility

Since we started, we have worked with communities and schools to support children who face some of the biggest barriers to learning.

The journey has been about more than numbers. It has been about bringing back possibility, and helping children get the tools, attention and encouragement they need to learn and grow.

  1. ’24

    Our journey begins

    We start working with communities and schools in Kiambu and Nakuru counties to support learners with disabilities.

  2. 332

    332 learners supported

    Learners with multiple disabilities get help to build early learning and inclusion, when they might otherwise have been left behind.

  3. ’26

    A focused step forward

    Two pilot projects, TAJI in Kajiado and INUKA in Nakuru, will support 96 children.

  4. What comes next

    Learning from the pilots so we can reach many more schools and children.
