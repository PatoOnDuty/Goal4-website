Pillar 1 · TAJI

ECDE Strengthening

We support early childhood centres with learning materials, teaching aids, teacher support and safe learning spaces, so every child can learn, grow and thrive.

We are partnering with the ECDE centre at Empeut Primary School. We will give learners a stronger start with play-based learning materials, modern teaching aids, teacher training and better classrooms. The goal is confident, capable learners.

Pillar 2 · INUKA

Disability Inclusion

We support children with disabilities with assistive learning resources, teacher training and inclusive classrooms, so every child can learn, grow and thrive.
