TAJI20 Jan 2026

TAJI ECDE pilot launches in Kajiado

We are partnering with the ECDE centre at Empeut Primary School to give 61 PP1 and PP2 learners a stronger start. That means play-based materials, modern teaching aids and support for the centre’s 2 teachers.

INUKA10 Feb 2026

INUKA adopts the Special Needs Unit at Mlimani

In Nakuru County, we are adopting the Special Needs Unit at Mlimani Integrated School. It serves 35 learners with multiple disabilities from Grade 1 to Grade 6, supported by 4 teachers.

Community5 Mar 2026

Introducing the Founders’ Circle

A growing community of Dream Builders helping 96 vulnerable children through school in 2026. Members get progress updates, event invites and field visits.
